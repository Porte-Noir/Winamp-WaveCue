# The Story Behind WaveCue

Winamp has survived decades of changes in digital music. Some consider it a relic of the past. To me, it remains a remarkable music player whose simplicity, flexibility and character deserve to be preserved.

After years of performing as a DJ under the name **Porte-Noir**, I wanted to continue using Winamp to prepare my sets and broadcast music.

I needed waveform visualization, customizable In/Out cue points, key and BPM analysis, and a practical way to organize musical selections.

Rather than abandoning Winamp for modern DJ software, I decided to give it the features I needed.

That's how **WaveCue** was born, followed by **Set Planner**.

The project was developed through AI-assisted programming using **ChatGPT by OpenAI**. My experience as a DJ guided the design, testing and evolution of both tools.

As a French-speaking creator, I've also incorporated several French-language options into WaveCue.

The philosophy remains simple: modernize Winamp while preserving the qualities that made it special.

**Technology supports the DJ. Artistic decisions remain human.**
